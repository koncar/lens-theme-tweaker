# Changelog

What changed in each version of this extension, newest first.

## 0.1.0

- Theme Tweaker as an extension: eleven preset themes, a quick palette and every color slot of Lens to edit live, separate dark and light palettes, saved themes, and JSON export and import, on a page of its own in Preferences › Extensions.
- A status bar item shows while Theme Tweaker's colors are on, and opens its page.
